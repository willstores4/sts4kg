tools/embed_trust.py embeds complete CRLF PEM records into the app and daemon.
The native HTTP context registers an array of SslPem pointers, retaining the
records and backing strings for its entire lifetime. HTTPS requests use the
keepalive connection path, retaining the custom native trust context.
Certificate, hostname and validity checks remain enabled.
